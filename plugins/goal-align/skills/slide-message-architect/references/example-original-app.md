# High-rated example: original app training

## Source challenge

A long spoken brief contained these intertwined ideas:

- A clinic's own app can improve as AI improves.
- The audience doubts whether they can build an app.
- Building from zero is genuinely difficult.
- A dental analogy makes that difficulty intuitive.
- The course starts from a completed app and spends roughly twelve hours customizing it.
- A post-orthodontic TBI analogy explains reproducibility.
- A shared completed codebase improves through feedback from many clinics.

Reference slides used dense but readable blue line-art layouts with two-card comparisons and three-card processes.

## Approved decomposition

Seven slides were preferred:

1. Future value: the original app grows with AI.
2. Audience doubt: can we really build it ourselves?
3. Honest limitation: not everyone can build from zero.
4. Problem analogy: promising identical perfect orthodontic results for every oral condition is impossible.
5. Method: copy a completed app in 10–20 minutes and spend the remaining time customizing it.
6. Solution analogy: start from the completed state and adapt it like post-orthodontic TBI.
7. Repeatability: a completed shared codebase becomes stronger through repeated use and feedback.

## Why seven worked

- Six would have overloaded the honest limitation and dental problem analogy.
- Eight would have separated the course method from its time allocation even though one three-step process could express both.
- The problem analogy and solution analogy stayed separate because they changed different beliefs.

## User feedback

The user explicitly rated both the decomposition and the resulting seven slides highly.
